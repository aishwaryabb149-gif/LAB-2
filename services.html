Navy

The Navy is the maritime defense force of a country responsible for protecting sea borders and ensuring security across oceans. It operates ships, submarines and aircraft to perform defense, surveillance, rescue missions and disaster relief operations. The Navy plays a vital role in safeguarding trade routes, maintaining maritime peace and strengthening national security. The navy operates warships, submarines, and aircraft to maintain naval dominance and conduct surveillance across oceans. It also participates in international exercises and peacekeeping missions to strengthen global maritime cooperation. The Indian Navy focuses on modernization, advanced technology, and training to maintain strong naval power and national security.
